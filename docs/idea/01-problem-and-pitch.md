# 01: Problem and pitch

## The problem, in plain words
Every business, from a 2-person agency to a 200-person manufacturer, has the same money chores:

- **Books:** every rupee in and out has to be recorded and categorised, with a bill or receipt behind it.
- **Invoices:** work done has to be invoiced: correctly, on time, to the right legal entity.
- **Collections:** clients have to be chased until they pay.
- **Tax:** you have to know what you owe, set it aside, and keep proof for every expense you claim.

Today this is done by:
1. **The owner, at midnight**, with a spreadsheet and a shoebox of receipts, or
2. **An accountant (CA) they see once a month or once a year**, who gets a pile of chaos, charges for the time spent sorting it, and only tells you about problems months later.

Nobody needs to be convinced of this. Every founder knows the year-end panic: hunting for invoices, lost bills, "you can't claim this, there's no receipt", and the client who never got invoiced.

## What's actually lost
- **Money:** work that was never invoiced, clients who paid late or never, expenses that can't be claimed because the bill is missing, tax surprises.
- **Time:** owners doing data entry instead of running the business.
- **Visibility:** you only learn last quarter's numbers when it's too late to act.

## The pitch
> **"Munshi is your business's personal CA: it keeps your books, makes your invoices, chases your money and tells you what you owe in tax, every day, not once a year."**

*Munshi (मुंशी): the traditional clerk-accountant who kept a business's books by hand. We're building the AI one.*

## Who it's for
| Segment | Their pain | Why Munshi fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small service businesses** (agencies, consultancies, studios, freelancers with a company) | Owner does the books; invoices go out late; receipts lost | Primary target: high pain, quick to onboard |
| **Growing SMBs** (10–200 people) with a part-time accountant | Monthly close takes days; accountant works from chaos | Munshi prepares a clean, explained close for the accountant |
| **CA / accounting firms** | Each client sends a mess every month | Later: one Munshi per client, the CA reviews instead of re-typing |

## What Munshi is NOT
- Not a replacement for a licensed professional signing your tax return. Munshi does the work **and shows its evidence**; a human signs.
- Not another accounting app you have to fill in. **You drop in what you have; Munshi does the rest.**
- Not a CFO. A CA keeps the books **correct**; a CFO uses them to **plan**. That's why Munshi feeds **cfo.ai** (see [05-sponsors.md](05-sponsors.md)).

## The 5-second test
Can a judge understand it instantly, with no country-specific knowledge? **"An AI accountant that closes your books every month and proves every number."** ✅
